Privacy Policy

BeatBuddy Song Matching app

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

BeatBuddy Song Matching app is an unofficial companion application designed to help BeatBuddy users find matching drum beats and recommended BPM values for songs.

This application is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Singular Sound.

2. Information We Collect

BeatBuddy Song Matching app does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

When you search for a song or artist, the search text is sent to our backend service in order to find matching song information.

3. Backend Service

The application uses Supabase as its backend database service. Search requests are sent to Supabase to retrieve song information such as artist, song title, BeatBuddy beat, and BPM.

For more information about Supabase's privacy practices, please refer to Supabase's Privacy Policy .

4. Data Storage

The application does not provide user accounts or user profiles and does not intentionally store personal information submitted by users.

The song database contains song-matching information used by the application.

5. Advertising

BeatBuddy Song Matching app does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising networks.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Search requests may be transmitted to the application's backend service, Supabase, solely to provide the requested search functionality.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the developer through the application's project repository.
